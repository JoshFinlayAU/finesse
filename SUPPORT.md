# Getting help with Finesse

If something isn't working, or you'd like to suggest a feature, please open an issue at https://github.com/JoshFinlayAU/finesse/issues. It helps to include your Apple TV model, your tvOS and Jellyfin server versions, and what you were trying to play.

You can also email support@athenanetworks.com.au, or call 1300 409 252 from within Australia. Finesse is published by Athena Networks, Parcel Locker 10303 80643, 139 Brisbane Road, Booval QLD 4304, Australia.

Finesse needs a Jellyfin server that you run or have access to. For questions about the server itself, Jellyfin's community is the best place to ask: https://jellyfin.org/contact
